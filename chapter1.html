THE BEGINNING

A pillar of blinding light descended from the sky, illuminating everything around it.

Within the light, several children were trapped, unable to understand what was happening.

The world around them seemed to disappear as the mysterious light continued to grow stronger.

"Something had awakened."
FIVE YEARS LATER

Five years had passed.

The world had changed.

What once seemed familiar had become something entirely different.

In a barren land, surrounded by silence and destruction, a young man slowly opened his eyes.

His name was Ryzo Kaveh.

And this was the beginning of his journey.

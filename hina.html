Name: Hina

Role: Ryzo's Sister

Story: The Vessel: Awakening

About Hina

Hina is Ryzo Kaveh's sister and one of the important people in his life.

She has a kind and caring nature and shares a strong bond with her brother.

Although she may appear ordinary at first, there is more to Hina than what she initially reveals.

Personality

Hina is caring, kind-hearted and emotionally connected to the people around her.

She cares deeply about her family and especially about Ryzo.

Despite the dangers surrounding their world, Hina tries to remain strong for the people she loves.

Character Details

  • Ryzo Kaveh's Sister
  • Kind-hearted
  • Strong Emotional Bond with Ryzo
  • Connected to the Story's Mysteries

Role in the Story

Hina's relationship with Ryzo makes her an important part of his journey.

Her presence also connects different parts of the story and adds another layer to the mysteries surrounding Ryzo and the world of The Vessel.

Mystery

There are still many things about Hina that remain unknown.

Her true connection to the deeper mysteries of the story will be revealed as the story progresses.

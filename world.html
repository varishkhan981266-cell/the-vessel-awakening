THE WORLD

Discover the world of The Vessel: Awakening

🌍 World Overview

The world of The Vessel: Awakening is shaped by mysterious energy known as Urja.

Warriors, creatures and vessels exist throughout this world, each connected to Urja in different ways.

⚡ Urja

Urja is a mysterious form of energy that flows through living beings. It can be controlled through breathing, concentration and training.

However, using too much Urja can place enormous strain on both the body and mind.

🔥 Types of Urja

  • Sarir Urja — Enhances physical strength, speed and endurance.
  • Drishti Urja — Allows special abilities related to vision and perception.
  • Astra Urja — Allows users to summon and control weapons.
  • Time Urja — A rare form of Urja connected to the manipulation of time.

👹 Bogeys

Bogeys are dangerous creatures that threaten human settlements.

Their origins and true nature remain mysterious, and stronger Bogeys can possess abilities far beyond ordinary creatures.

🔴 Vessels

Vessels are individuals connected to powers and destinies that ordinary people cannot understand.

The true meaning of a Vessel is one of the mysteries at the heart of the story.
